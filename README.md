## Overview
An entry for the make.fun competition **"Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human"**. This program generates a deadpan, highly specific 1-star review written by an unimpressed house cat about its human caretaker.

## Evaluation Rounds & Candidate Scoring

We drafted and evaluated 3 candidate reviews against the judge's 5 criteria:
1. Funny 1-star cat review about human owner
2. Specific and recognisable complaint (food bowl, closed door, laptop keyboard)
3. Unmistakably feline voice (human measured by cat standards)
4. Tight, surprising wording with a turn/sting in the final words
5. Deadpan shape of a real 1-star review with absurd feline logic

### Candidate 1 (Breakfast Timing)
*Line:* `1/5 stars. Hairless primate opened eyes at 7:02 AM instead of 7:00 AM, causing two minutes of existential terror where I considered eating dry kibble like a common feral. Unreliable staff.`
*Scores:* (1) 5/5, (2) 4/5 (food/kibble), (3) 5/5, (4) 4/5, (5) 5/5.

### Candidate 2 (Closed Bathroom Door)
*Line:* `1/5 stars. Giant biped locks itself in the tiled chamber for twenty minutes without supervision, then emerges completely dry. I screamed at the wood until my throat bled, yet it offered zero mice. Terrible management.`
*Scores:* (1) 5/5, (2) 5/5 (closed door/tiled room), (3) 5/5, (4) 4/5, (5) 5/5.

### Candidate 3 (Laptop Keyboard / Winner & Polished)
*Line:* `1/5 stars. Hairless primate stares at glowing rectangle all day instead of warming my lap. When I walked across the keyboard to save its soul, it moved me with two hands. Unacceptable service.`
*Scores:* (1) 5/5, (2) 5/5 (glowing rectangle / keyboard), (3) 5/5, (4) 5/5 (turn: "moved me with two hands. Unacceptable service."), (5) 5/5.

## Final Entry
Length: 192 characters (within the 1-200 char limit).
Deterministic, zero dependencies, pure Python.
